FAQ

Questions? We Got You.

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

Do I need prior gym experience to join? +

Absolutely not. Whether you've never lifted a dumbbell or you're a seasoned athlete, our trainers will guide you from day one. We build a workout plan around your current fitness level.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes! Your first session is completely free. Walk in for a trial workout, tour the facility, meet our trainers and see if we're a fit — no pressure, no commitment.

What are your opening hours? +

We're open 24/7 for our members. Whether you like to train at 5 AM before work or 2 AM after a late shift, the doors are always open for you.

Do you offer personal training? +

Yes — one-on-one personal training is available with all our certified coaches. PT sessions can be purchased as add-ons to any membership, or included in our Elite plan.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership? +

Yes. You can freeze your membership for up to 30 days per year (perfect for travel or illness). Cancellations are easy and there are no hidden fees or lock-in periods.

Is there a joining fee? +

No joining fee, no admission fee, no hidden charges. You pay only the plan price you see — that's it. We believe in transparent pricing.
